This is the first tech & meet, hosted by Howest on Tuesdays, that I attended. The talk itself didn't last too long as most of the time was reserved for the networking afterwards, an important part of any tech & meet.

Typically you'd find people focused on security filling out the seats. I myself am a Software Engineer and not a Cybersecurity Expert, yet I don't feel like that should stop me from learning outside of my field. That's why the talk from Dieter Sarrazyn, an employee at Secudea which provides cyber security services to industrial entities and their operations, appealed to me. It was something completely new.

Despite my eagerness to learn, the talk itself didn't go quite as I expected. Without the proper background and experience it was easy to get lost within the technical details. Parts of it were easy to follow while some parts were rather confusing. I am certain those of a more security oriented mindset followed along nicely, but as someone more pre-occupied with software development, I struggled. The talk was very technical and due to the afformentioned issues, I couldn't learn much. Most of the concepts I had no previous contact with.

For the future I hope they give a sort of small explanation before the presentation, something that gives a basic overview of the topic and allows the listeners to prepare. If you're interested in security, I definitely recommend such talks. I myself would not attend it again, not without the proper knowledge to listen. It is also not something I would learn more for as my intended expertise still lies within software development.
