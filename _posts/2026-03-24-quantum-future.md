This is the latest tech & meet, hosted by Howest on Tuesdays, that I've attended. It was a long but interesting look into quantum computing, its impact on security & encryption together with predictions of when certain algorithms will be cracked.

This talk is of interest for anyone with even basic knowledge of IT, as its influence is wide reaching. Almost all systems will need to adapt to the coming of quantum computing, and certain outdated software and services will definitely feel the impact of old encryption methods becoming deprecated. Sarah Ampe, the speaker, is a leading expert in cryptography with a lot of knowledge in mathematics.

There was a mix of technical details, such as a short exploration in how quantom computing worked, and why it had certain limitations. Outside of that there was also a plethora of non technical information, such as practical applications of quantum computing, the quantum threat and how it'll impact encryption and an estimated timeline of when certain breakthroughs will come and changes are needed. Even if the technical details are too complex, there's still a lot to be gained here. 

Aside from the explanation of async encryption, something I've had to study already, a lot of it was new and very interesting, making for a very positive experience. I learned quite a lot, and although quantum computing is still something I worry about it, I am glad to know proper actions are being taken to handle it.

I recommend this tech & meet to everyone with an interest in quantum computing, security or encryptions. I personally would attend again, if only to learn more about quantum computing itself. This all falls outside of my area of expertise, however, and I doubt I'll apply any of the knowledge myself.
