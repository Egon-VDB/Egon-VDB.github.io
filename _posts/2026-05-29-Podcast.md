This podcast, recorded in Dutch, was made for the Professional Networking module. In this podcast I discuss the development of software for hospitals, the rules and governing bodies around such and the use of AI within it. My guest speaker is Adriaan de Saeger, developer at Comeo, where they develop Comeocare for hospitals.

The podcast starts with a simple introduction of my guest, who he is, what he studied and where he works. Afterwards we immediately dive into some of the rules of developing software for hospitals. What's particulary interesting is how these rules get enforced, the organization that check this and all the audits that happen to make it all progress smoothly. Also covered is the process of integrating legacy systems and deploying the software, as most hospitals have their own environments and software.

Attention is also placed on the use of AI, what you can't and can do. Adriaan goes into how he uses AI, what he does to make sure its info is correct, and which AI best suited his needs.
