This is the third tech & meet, hosted by Howest on Tuesdays, that I attended. The talk itself was longer this time as there was a lot more to cover.

As opposed to the first tak, this was one that could be of interest to anyone within the IT sphere. We all work with networks, connecting systems and transfering files. As such the transition to IPv6 is something we all benefit from being more knowledgable of. The talk was given by Nico, a teacher at Howest, who is quite knowledgable about such topics and security.

The talk itself was a good mix of technical details with more broader concepts. I learned a lot from the history of IPv4, the problems it had and the patchwork solutions we made. How and why we should transition was also a big focus, and while I already agreed, I am now a lot more convinced on the need to transition to IPv6.

I experienced the talk as an overall positive event with a lot of knowledge to be gained. I had learned about IPv6 before but never in such detail. It's easy to dismiss the faults of IPv4 but to see them laid out so clearly, it drove the point of the transition and the need for it.

I personally believe this to be an excellent example of a tech & meet and it is one that I recommend to anyone with an interest in IT. I'd definitely attend again and this is information that I plan to take with me to all the workplaces I enter in the future.
