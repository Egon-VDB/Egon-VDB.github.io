Hack the Future is a yearly hackathon that runs for two days. I myself participated on the first day on the 12th of November together with my teammate [Marwah][MARWAH]. I had such a blast with the challenge that I am looking forward to participating in future years.

## The event
The event took place in Antwerp where several clients presented various challenges covering a variety of topics and tech stacks. I personally looked for challenges that involved full-stack development, hoping to improve my skills there.

I also visited the zoo, as it was included in the challenge, together with my teammate. We decided to spend our lunchbreak there and had a lot of fun looking at all the animals and exhibitions. 

Hack the Future lasted from the morning, where the groups were divided and led to their respective rooms, to the evening, where we all ate a buffet of delicious food as the winners were announced. By the end we all took the train home as we discussed the challenges we all tackled.

## The challenge
The challenge I tackled was 104, given to us by Collide. We had to develop a backend that generated coding challenges, while also providing a front-end to fetch and solve those challenges. The backend was written in Java, while the front-end ran on Vite, both things I had experience with. it is due to that experience that we managed to complete the challenge, as writing both a front- and backend in one day is no easy feat.

We divided the challenge between us and I took it upon myself to develop the front-end, something I love doing. There's something rewarding about developing modular components that get re-used across your website, cutting your development time in half. By the end of the challenge we had a good result, but we sadly didn't win.

## The awards
We didn't win any awards sadly, as we won neither the jury vote or the vote from our fellow participants. As we looked at all the winners, however, we knew that we gave the challenge a good try and that we could be proud of ourselves.
