On the 12th of November, I attended Aquatopia, a hackathon organized by Hack the Future in Antwerp. During the event, I had the opportunity to collaborate closely with my teammate [Marwah][MARWAH] while also connecting with friends and other developers throughout the day. The energetic atmosphere made the experience incredibly enjoyable, and it left me eager to participate in more hackathons in the future.

Alongside the event itself, we were also given access to Antwerp Zoo, which provided a welcome break during lunch. After spending hours coding, it was refreshing to step away from our laptops for a while and explore the zoo, seeing all the animals on display.
