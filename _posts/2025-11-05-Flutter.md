This is the second tech & meet, hosted by Howest on Tuesdays, that I attended. The talk was very extensive giving a lot of information in a short time window.

This talk is of interest to anyone wishing to develop websites and apps, and even the aspiring game developer. Everyone wishing to develop their app using a single codebase for multiple platforms is sure to want to try out flutter after this tech & meet. The speaker was Thijs Pirmez, an expert on flutter and someone who has worked with it extensively.

The talk itself didn't focus on the technical details of using flutter. Instead it focused on all the benefits that came with flutter, the different tools it gave and all the platforms it could be used for. By far the biggest boon was the cross-platform development using only one codebase. This simplifies development so much it's insane. I have yet to use flutter myself so I don't know the extent of its capabilities in cross-platform development yet, but I am certain I'll come in contact with it again in the future.

I found the talk to be a great experience with lots of easy to digest knowledge. I've learned capabilities of flutter that I couldn't even imagine, such as a 2D game engine to develop games in flutter. I also learned the basics of how flutter works and how to utilize it.

I recommend this topic to anyone and would definitely attend the tech & meet again. Although I have no plans to develop an app for multiple platforms yet, but the game engine is definitely something I'll tinker with in the future.
