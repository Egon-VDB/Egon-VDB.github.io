This project is smaller in scope than most of my other work, but it is one I deeply love sharing. When Baldur's Gate 3 launched out I was absolutely hooked. I always love RPGs with meaningful choices and character creation.

Despite that, though, I felt something was missing. I’ve always had a deep love for gothic horror, especially vampires, yet the closest the game offered was a vampire companion. So I decided to take matters into my own hands, creating my own dhampir mod, and bringing that experience into the world of Baldur's Gate 3.

## The mod
The mod itself adds a completely new playable race that can be selected during character creation. The race includes access to elf and half-elf customization options, a modified vampiric bite that scales with Constitution, and an adapted version of the drow ability progression to support its dark, supernatural theme.

I plan to continue expanding the mod with the goal of eventually releasing it publicly for others to enjoy. Before that, though, there are still a few rough edges to polish. I want to create custom character heads featuring proper vampiric fangs, refine some lingering gameplay issues, and design a unique icon to better represent the race in-game.

## First iteration
When Larian Studios released their modding toolkit for Baldur's Gate 3, I was thrilled. At last, I had the chance to add playable vampirism to the game myself. I installed the toolkit and immediately started digging through documentation and tutorials on custom race creation. Within just a few hours, I had created my first personal dhampir mod.

It was far from polished. I had assigned tags that, while thematically appropriate, blocked certain spells, many character creation options were missing, and the race itself was too strong. However, that rough first prototype taught me a tremendous amount, and the lessons I learned from it became the foundation for a far more polished and refined dhampir mod.

## A refined approach
I went back to the drawing board, taking a slower and more methodical approach to the mod. Like many modders, I aimed to combine existing assets in creative ways to build something new. I began by listing every ability and asset I would need, focusing especially on darkness-based powers and Astarion’s bite attack. From there, I carefully mapped out each step of the process to make sure I wouldn’t overlook anything important.

After an afternoon of tinkering, testing, and plenty of head-scratching, I finally produced a much stronger iteration of the mod. I adapted Astarion’s bite into a reusable natural weapon and even implemented proper attack rolls, replacing the original guaranteed hit mechanic with something that felt far more balanced and immersive. For the race’s additional abilities, I modified the powers used by the drow, drawing inspiration from their affinity for darkness, light sensitivity, and shadow-themed magic.

## What's next
There is still work to be done before this can be released as a public mod. A few graphical elements are missing, some asset assignments still need refinement, and the race currently lacks a unique icon. On top of that, I especially want to properly implement vampiric fangs into the character models to better capture the intended aesthetic.

Once these final improvements are in place, I plan to release the mod publicly for others to enjoy. Hopefully, fellow gothic horror enthusiasts will be able to use it to enhance their own Baldur's Gate 3 experience.
