his year I participated in Hack the Future, a two-day hackathon where teams tackle challenges from various clients. The event ran for two days and I particiated on the first day together with my teammate [Marwah][MARWAH]. I spent the day building a full-stack application, and it turned out to be even more fun and interesting than I expected.

## The event
The event took place in Antwerp, where several companies presented challenges covering a wide range of topics and technologies. It wasn't easy to pick our challenge, as so many of them spoke to my interests. Eventually we settled on a challenge that offered both backend and front-end development, something that could really test our skills.

The event didn't just have programming however. Included was a free visit to the zoo, something we took full advantage of to see all the amazing exhibitions on display.

Hack the Future lasted from the morning, where the groups were divided and led to their respective rooms, to the evening, where we all ate a buffet of delicious food as the winners were announced. By the end we all took the train home as we discussed the challenges we all tackled.

## The challenge
The challenge I tackled was 104, given to us by Collide. We had to develop a backend that generated coding challenges, while also providing a front-end to fetch and solve those challenges. The backend was written in Java, while the front-end ran on Vite, both things I had experience with. It was thanks to that experience that we managed to complete the challenge, as building both a frontend and backend in a single day is no easy feat.

We divided the challenge between us and I took it upon myself to develop the front-end, something I love doing. There's something rewarding about developing modular components that get re-used across your website, cutting your development time in half. By the end of the challenge we had a good result, but, unfortunately, we didn't win.

## The awards
Unfortunately, we didn't win any awards, as we won neither the jury vote or the vote from our fellow participants. Although we didn't win, the experience was incredibly valuable. Building a complete full-stack application in a single day pushed us to work efficiently, collaborate effectively, and make practical technical decisions under pressure. I'm already looking forward to participating again next year.
