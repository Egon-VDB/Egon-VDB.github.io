My name is Egon Van den Buys and I am a full-stack software engineer & artist passionate about learning and developing my skills. I try to be open to many things and not focus on specific aspects of art & development, so that I am always a versatile artist & developer.

## Education
I first started my higher education at [Digital Arts & Entertainment][DAE]. As someone with a fond love for all artistic endeavors, I signed up to follow the [Game Graphics Production][GGP] curriculum, and for those 3 exciting years I learned how to create several aspects of video game art. I've learned skills such as:
- Concept art
- Rendering
- 3D modeling of props & characters
- Game design
- Rigging

Currently I'm pursuing my 2nd degree in [Software Engineering][SE] at [Howest][HOWEST] because I want to learn how to develop software. I plan to be a full-stack developer while I create my own games in my spare time. I have a paritcular interest in:
- Computer networks
- Software architecture
- Entity relationship mapping

## Hobbies
I have a deep appreciation for older games, specifically the PS1 and PS2 era of gaming as well as the consoles of that time. My collection spans a lot of hardware and games from that era, with the hardware often being modded for homebrew features.

Outside of games, I also enjoy a good fantasy or sci-fi book, with R.E. Howard and H.P. Lovecraft being some of my favourite authors. Sometimes I even go on my own adventures by playing tabletop RPGs with my friends.

Lastly, I spend a lot of my free time doing creative work, from 3D models to digital art. I hope to one day be confident enough in my work to sell them at a convention or online.
